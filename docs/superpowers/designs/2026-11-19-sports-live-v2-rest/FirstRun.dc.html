When · the first time a profile opens Sports & Live. A profile that already changed My sports or follows a team is marked done silently.
Skippable everywhere · "Skip setup" on every step; Back on step 1 = skip. Skipping keeps today's defaults and lands on Live Now. It never asks again; Settings → Setup runs it again.
Saves as you go · each Next saves that step, so leaving halfway keeps what was picked.
One footer · fixed 80 px bar: Skip (ghost) · step dots · Back · Next (PRIMARY). Focus starts on the step's main control; ↓ reaches Next.
Step 2 · list + stage: sports left (check = SelectedTint + ✓), the focused sport's leagues right; → enters them. Next starts fetching those leagues' team lists for step 3.
Step 3 · the Team search, filter = the sports from step 2, empty field = browse A–Z. Step 4: three cards show a real row in each mode; delay is one control.
